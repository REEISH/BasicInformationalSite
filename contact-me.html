Though the Emperor now resides in the pantheon of history—beyond the reach of courier, diplomat, or earthly correspondence—his spirit remains easily accessible. You need only consult the immortal maxims he left behind. To contact the essence of Napoleon is to draw upon the relentless drive, courage, and ambition that continues to motivate humankind to this day.

On Perseverance: "Courage isn't having the strength to go on—it is going on when you don't have strength."
On Action: "Take time to deliberate, but when the time for action has arrived, stop thinking and go in."
On Leadership: "A leader is a dealer in hope."
On Ambition: "Impossible is a word to be found only in the dictionary of fools."
On Triumph: "Victory belongs to the most persevering."
